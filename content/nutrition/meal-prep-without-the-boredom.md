# Meal Prep Without the Boredom

Most meal prep fails for one reason: by Wednesday you cannot face the fourth
identical container. The fix is to prep **components**, not finished meals, and
assemble them differently through the week. The Meal Templates and Meal Prep
pages in this app are built for exactly this — save a recipe once, prep a batch,
and log portions from it as you eat them.

## The component method

Cook one or two things from each column. Combine at meal time.

| Protein | Carbohydrate | Vegetables | Sauces and extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sheet-pan chicken thighs | A pot of rice | Roasted peppers and onions | Salsa |
| Browned lean mince | Roast potatoes or sweet potatoes | Steamed broccoli | Pesto |
| Hard-boiled eggs | Cooked lentils | Raw slaw mix | Yogurt-garlic sauce |
| Baked tofu | Whole-wheat wraps (bought) | Bagged salad | Hot sauce, soy sauce |

The same chicken becomes a rice bowl with salsa on Monday, a wrap with slaw and
yogurt sauce on Tuesday, and a salad on Wednesday.

## A two-hour prep session

1. **0:00** — Oven on. Start the rice or lentils on the stove.
2. **0:10** — Season and tray up the protein; tray up the vegetables to roast. Both into the oven.
3. **0:20** — Eggs on to boil. While things cook, make one sauce and chop raw vegetables.
4. **0:45** — Pull things out as they finish. Let them cool briefly before lidding.
5. **1:00** — Portion proteins and carbohydrates into containers; keep sauces separate so things do not go soggy.
6. **1:15** — Clean as the last trays cool. Label containers with the date.

## Food safety basics

- Cool cooked food quickly and get it into the fridge promptly rather than leaving it on the counter for hours.
- Food-safety agencies generally advise eating refrigerated leftovers within a few days. Prep for about three to four days, and freeze anything meant for later in the week. (Check [foodsafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov) for the current guidance where you live.)
- Reheat until steaming hot all the way through.
- When in doubt, throw it out. Smell is not a reliable test.

## Making it stick

- Prep on the same day each week, and plan the week's meals before you shop.
- Start by prepping lunches only. Add more once lunches are automatic.
- Keep two or three "emergency" meals in the freezer or cupboard for weeks when the prep does not happen.
- Log from the prep, not from memory: a batch saved in Meal Prep records the same macros every time you eat a portion.
