# Calisthenics Routine: Beginner Full Body, 3× a Week

## Goal

Build a base across every pattern: pull, push, legs and core. Train three
non-consecutive days a week (for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday), about
45 minutes each. Each exercise is a **slot**. You fill it with whichever rung of
that ladder you are currently on.

## Equipment

- Pull-up bar
- Dip bars
- Push-up handles
- Chair or box (optional, for assisted rungs)

## Warm-up (8–10 minutes)

1. 2 minutes of easy movement: marching, jumping jacks, or dancing around.
2. Wrist circles, 10 each way; arm circles, 10 each way.
3. 10 bodyweight squats, 10 glute bridges.
4. Scapular pull-ups × 5 and dip-bar support hold × 15 seconds.
5. One easy set of the first exercise at an easier rung.

## The session

Pair the exercises (A1 then A2, and so on). Rest 60–90 seconds after each
exercise within a pair, and 2 minutes between pairs. Stop every set 1–2 reps
short of failure.

| Slot | Ladder | Sets × reps | Starting rung for most beginners |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Pull-ups (`pull-ups.md`) | 3 × 5–8 | Negatives or feet-assisted pull-ups |
| A2 | Dips (`dips.md`) | 3 × 5–8 | Support hold (3 × 20–30 s) or negatives |
| B1 | Rows (`rows.md`) | 3 × 8–12 | Steep incline row |
| B2 | Push-ups (`push-ups-on-handles.md`) | 3 × 8–12 | Incline or full push-up on handles |
| C1 | Legs, squat line (`legs.md`) | 3 × 8–12 each leg | Split squat |
| C2 | Legs, hinge line (`legs.md`) | 3 × 10–15 | Glute bridge or single-leg bridge |
| D | Core (`core-on-bars.md`) | 3 × 8–12 or 3 × 15–20 s | Hanging knee raise, or tuck L-sit hold |

Log each session in the Workout page so you can see reps rising over time.

## Cool-down (5 minutes)

- Dead hang, 20–30 seconds, relaxed.
- Doorway or dip-bar chest stretch, 30 seconds each side.
- Hip flexor stretch, 30 seconds each side.
- Child's pose, 60 seconds.

## Progression

- **Within a rung:** add reps until you reach the top of the rep range on all 3 sets.
- **Between rungs:** once you meet the rung's "move up when" standard, move that slot up one rung next session (see the section README).
- **Regress** a slot if you can't get 3 clean reps.
- After 4–8 weeks, or when progress stalls, take a **deload week** at half the sets. When most slots are two or three rungs up and sessions are running long, move to the intermediate push/pull/legs routine.
