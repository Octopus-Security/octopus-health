# Calisthenics Routine: Intermediate Push / Pull / Legs

## Goal

More volume per movement pattern than the full-body routine allows. This is
for trainees who can already do at least 3 × 8 strict pull-ups, 3 × 10 dips and
3 × 12 push-ups. Run it as push, pull and legs across six days with a rest day
(P/P/L/P/P/L/rest), or three days a week (P/P/L) if recovery is limited.

## Equipment

- Pull-up bar
- Dip bars
- Push-up handles
- Chair or box (optional), a backpack with books for load (optional), a cushion (optional)

## Warm-up (10 minutes, every day)

1. 3 minutes of easy movement.
2. Joint circles: wrists, elbows, shoulders, hips, knees, ankles.
3. For push days: support hold 2 × 20 seconds, 10 incline push-ups on the dip bars, wrist rocks.
4. For pull days: dead hang 30 seconds, 2 × 8 scapular pull-ups.
5. For leg days: 15 squats, 10 split squats each leg, 10 glute bridges.

## The session

Each exercise is the rung you're on in that ladder. Rest 2–3 minutes on the
first exercise of the day and 60–90 seconds on the rest.

**Push day**
| Exercise | Ladder | Sets × reps |
|---|---|---|
| Dips | `dips.md` | 4 × 6–10 |
| Pike / handstand push-ups | `pike-and-handstand-push-ups.md` | 4 × 5–8 |
| Push-ups on handles (deficit, archer or pseudo-planche) | `push-ups-on-handles.md` | 3 × 6–12 |
| L-sit | `core-on-bars.md` | 3 × 10–20 s |

**Pull day**
| Exercise | Ladder | Sets × reps |
|---|---|---|
| Pull-ups (full, L-sit, archer) | `pull-ups.md` | 4 × 5–8 |
| Rows | `rows.md` | 4 × 8–12 |
| Chin-ups | `chin-ups.md` | 3 × 6–10 |
| Hanging leg raise / toes-to-bar | `core-on-bars.md` | 3 × 8–12 |

**Legs day**
| Exercise | Ladder | Sets × reps |
|---|---|---|
| Squat line (Bulgarian, shrimp, pistol) | `legs.md` | 4 × 6–10 each leg |
| Single-leg Romanian deadlift | `legs.md` | 3 × 10 each leg |
| Nordic curl or single-leg bridge | `legs.md` | 3 × 3–5 / 3 × 12 |
| Windshield wipers or hanging leg raise | `core-on-bars.md` | 3 × 6–10 |

## Cool-down (5–8 minutes)

- Push days: chest and front-shoulder stretch, wrist stretches.
- Pull days: dead hang, lat stretch, biceps stretch against a wall.
- Leg days: hamstring, hip flexor and calf stretches.
- 1 minute of slow breathing.

## Progression

- **Double progression inside each rung:** work up to the top of the rep range on all sets, then move up a rung (per the section README standard) and restart at the bottom of the range.
- **Optional load:** once a rung's top reps are easy but the next rung is too big a jump, add a small load in a backpack. This works on dips, chin-ups and pistols.
- **Deload** every 4–8 weeks at half the sets. With six training days, watch your elbows and shoulders closely. Drop to three days if joints ache.
