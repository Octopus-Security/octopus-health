# Calisthenics Routine: Grease the Groove, 20 Minutes a Day

## Goal

"Grease the groove" means practising a movement often, in easy sets well short
of failure, spread across the day. The aim is to get better at that movement
through frequent quality practice rather than exhausting sessions. It suits
busy days, and people who want more pull-ups or dips without a long workout.
Pick **one or two** movements for a block of 2–4 weeks.

## Equipment

- Pull-up bar and/or dip bars, somewhere you walk past often
- Push-up handles

## Warm-up (1–2 minutes, before each mini-set)

The sets are easy, but cold joints still need a moment:

- 10 arm circles each way and a few wrist circles.
- For pull-ups: a 10-second dead hang and 3 scapular pulls.
- For dips: a 10-second support hold.
- For push-ups: 5 incline push-ups on the dip bars.

## The session

1. **Find your number.** On the first day, do one fresh, clean max set of the chosen exercise (your current rung). Stop when form breaks.
2. **Mini-sets:** through the day, do sets of **about half that number**. For example, with a max of 8 pull-ups, do sets of 4.
3. **Frequency:** 5–8 mini-sets, spaced at least 15–30 minutes apart. Each mini-set plus its warm-up takes 2–3 minutes, about 20 minutes of work across the whole day.
4. **Never to failure.** Every rep should be crisp. If a set feels hard, end the day's practice.

Example day with pull-ups (max 8) and push-ups on handles (max 16):

| Time | Mini-set |
|---|---|
| Morning | 4 pull-ups, 8 push-ups |
| Late morning | 4 pull-ups |
| Lunch | 4 pull-ups, 8 push-ups |
| Afternoon | 4 pull-ups |
| Early evening | 4 pull-ups, 8 push-ups |
| Evening | 4 pull-ups |

## Cool-down (2–3 minutes, end of day)

- Dead hang, 20–30 seconds.
- Chest stretch against the dip bars or a doorway, 30 seconds each side.
- Wrist stretches.

## Progression

- **Retest** every 1–2 weeks with a single fresh max set, and set the mini-set size to half the new number.
- When your max reaches the "move up when" standard of your rung (see the ladder file), move up a rung and restart with that rung's max.
- **Take 1–2 days off per week.** Grease the groove works through frequency, but your elbows and shoulders still need rest days.
- Avoid pairing grease-the-groove on a movement with heavy training of the same movement in the same week. Choose one or the other.
- Stop and deload if elbows or shoulders ache. High-frequency pulling is a common cause of elbow irritation.
