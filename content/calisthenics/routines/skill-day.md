# Calisthenics Routine: Skill Day

## Goal

Practise skills while fresh, with short, high-quality attempts. Run this once
or twice a week alongside another routine, in place of one of its sessions.
Pick **two or three skills** per session, not all of them. The volume here is
kept deliberately low because straight-arm work loads the tendons (see
`skills-and-statics.md`).

## Equipment

- Pull-up bar (with room for levers and muscle-ups, if you train those)
- Dip bars
- Push-up handles
- A clear wall, a cushion (optional), a chair or box (optional)

## Warm-up (12–15 minutes)

Longer than usual, because the joints matter more than the muscles here.

1. 3 minutes of easy movement.
2. Wrist circles and rocks, 1 minute. Elbow circles, arm circles.
3. Scapular pull-ups × 10, scapular push-ups on the handles × 10.
4. Support hold on the dip bars, 2 × 20 seconds.
5. For each skill you'll train: 2 easy sets at the rung below.

## The session

Choose two or three blocks. Rest 90 seconds to 2 minutes between attempts. You
are practising, not getting tired.

| Block | Skill (ladder) | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Handstand | Wall handstand hold (`pike-and-handstand-push-ups.md`) | 5 × 20–30 s |
| L-sit | Current L-sit rung (`core-on-bars.md`) | 5 × 10–15 s |
| Front lever | Current front lever rung (`skills-and-statics.md`) | 5 × 5–10 s |
| Planche line | Crow or tuck planche on handles (`skills-and-statics.md`) | 5 × 5–10 s |
| Back lever | Skin-the-cat or tuck back lever (`skills-and-statics.md`) | 3–4 × 3 slow reps or 5–10 s |
| Muscle-up | Current rung: chest-to-bar, negatives, or strict (`skills-and-statics.md`) | 5 × 1–3 |

Finish with one strength exercise that supports the skills, such as full dips
or pull-ups, for 2–3 sets.

## Cool-down (5–8 minutes)

- Wrist flexor and extensor stretches, 30 seconds each.
- Biceps stretch against a wall, 30 seconds each side.
- Dead hang, 30 seconds.
- Pike or pancake stretch on the floor, 60 seconds.

## Progression

- Increase hold time within a rung until you meet the standard, then move up a rung.
- Add a set only after holds are consistently good. Don't add both at once.
- Stop the session early if form drops across two attempts in a row.
- Any elbow, biceps-tendon or wrist ache that lasts into the next day means halving skill volume for a week.
