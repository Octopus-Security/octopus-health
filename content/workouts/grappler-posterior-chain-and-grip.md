# Grappler's Posterior Chain and Grip

## Goal

Build the strength that grappling demands but rarely trains directly: a strong
hinge, a back that can hold posture under a pull, and hands that do not give out
halfway through a round. This is a strength and conditioning session for the
gym, not a mat-drill session.

## Equipment

- Barbell and plates
- Pull-up bar
- Dumbbells or kettlebells for carries
- A towel (or a gi jacket) for grip work
- Optional: a sled, or a rope for pulling

## Warm-up (10 minutes)

1. 3–5 minutes on a rower or bike, easy.
2. 10 cat-cows, 10 bird dogs each side, 10 glute bridges.
3. 10 band pull-aparts and 10 scapular pull-ups.
4. Ramp-up sets for the deadlift: empty bar × 8, then 2–3 progressively heavier sets of 3–5 before your working weight.

## The session

| Block | Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Trap-bar or conventional deadlift | 4 × 4–5 | 2–3 min |
| B1 | Towel (or gi) pull-ups | 4 × as many as clean | 90 s |
| B2 | Barbell or chest-supported row | 4 × 8 | 90 s |
| C1 | Single-leg Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8 each leg | 60 s |
| C2 | Suitcase carry (one heavy dumbbell) | 3 × 30 m each side | 60 s |
| D | Towel dead hang | 3 × max hold | 60 s |

Leave one or two reps in reserve on the deadlift. You are building strength for
the sport, not testing a max the week of a tournament.

## Cool-down (6 minutes)

- Dead hang with a regular grip, 20–30 seconds.
- Pigeon or figure-four stretch, 45 seconds each side.
- Hamstring stretch lying on your back with a strap, 45 seconds each side.
- Wrist flexor and extensor stretches, 30 seconds each.

## Progression

- **Deadlift:** add a small amount of weight each week while all reps stay crisp. When bar speed slows noticeably, repeat the weight the following week before adding again.
- **Towel pull-ups:** add a rep per set; once you reach 8 clean reps on every set, add a small weight or slow the lowering to 4 seconds.
- **Carries:** first add distance, then add weight.
- **Towel hang:** add 5 seconds per set per week.

Schedule this away from your hardest mat days (for example, not the day before
hard sparring). Grip and lower back are shared between this session and
grappling, and fatigue there carries over.
