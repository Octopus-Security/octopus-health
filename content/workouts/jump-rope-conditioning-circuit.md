# Jump Rope Conditioning Circuit

## Goal

Improve work capacity — the ability to keep producing effort and recover
between bursts — in half an hour. Rope work also trains rhythm, footwork and
calf/ankle stiffness, which carries over to striking sports.

## Equipment

- A jump rope sized so the handles reach roughly armpit height when you stand on the middle
- A flat surface with some give (wood, rubber, packed ground). Avoid bare concrete if you can
- A round timer

## Warm-up (6–8 minutes)

1. 2 minutes of easy rope (basic bounce), or marching if you are new to the rope.
2. Ankle circles and 20 slow calf raises.
3. 10 bodyweight squats, 10 walking lunges, 10 arm swings each direction.
4. 1 minute of rope at a slightly quicker pace.

## The session

Five rounds. Each round is 4 minutes of work, then 1 minute of rest:

| Minute | Work |
|---|---|
| 1 | Jump rope, steady pace |
| 2 | 10 push-ups, 15 air squats, then plank for the rest of the minute |
| 3 | Jump rope, faster: alternate 20 seconds hard / 10 seconds easy |
| 4 | 10 reverse lunges each leg, then mountain climbers for the rest of the minute |
| 5 | Rest — walk, breathe slowly, sip water |

Aim to finish round five at the same quality as round one. If you are
noticeably slower or sloppier by round three, the pace was too high; that is
useful information, not failure.

## Cool-down (5 minutes)

- 2 minutes walking until breathing settles.
- Calf stretch against a wall, 30 seconds each side (bent-knee and straight-knee).
- Standing quad stretch, 30 seconds each side.
- Slow nasal breathing, 1 minute.

## Progression

Progress in this order, one change per week:

1. Add a round (up to seven).
2. Cut the rest minute to 45 seconds, then 30.
3. Replace the steady rope minute with footwork variations (side-to-side, boxer skip, high knees).
4. Add a light weighted vest or a heavier rope only once the above feels routine.

Jumping volume adds up quickly on the shins and Achilles tendons. If either is
sore the next morning, hold the volume where it is for another week.
