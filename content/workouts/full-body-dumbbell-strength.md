# Full-Body Dumbbell Strength

## Goal

Build general strength across every major movement pattern (squat, hinge,
push, pull, carry) in about 50 minutes. This is the session to run when the gym
is crowded, when you train at home, or when you want a lighter-on-the-joints
day than barbell work.

## Equipment

- A pair of dumbbells you can adjust (or a small rack of fixed pairs)
- A flat bench, or a sturdy box/step
- Optional: a timer (the app's Timers page works)

## Warm-up (8–10 minutes)

1. 3 minutes of easy movement to get warm: jump rope, brisk walk, or bike.
2. 10 bodyweight squats, slow on the way down.
3. 10 hip hinges with hands on hips (push the hips back, flat back).
4. 10 push-ups (from knees if needed) and 10 band pull-aparts or arm circles.
5. One light "ramp" set of the first exercise at roughly half your working weight.

The ramp set is the important one: it rehearses the exact movement you are
about to load, which a generic warm-up does not.

## The session

Rest 90–120 seconds between sets of the paired lifts (A, B). Rest less on C.

| Block | Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Goblet squat | 4 × 8 | Elbows inside knees at the bottom, chest up |
| A2 | Dumbbell bench press | 4 × 8 | Shoulder blades pinned back, control the lowering |
| B1 | Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 3 × 10 | Soft knees, push hips back until you feel the hamstrings |
| B2 | One-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 10 each side | Pull the elbow toward the hip, not the ear |
| C1 | Reverse lunge | 3 × 8 each leg | Step back, lower under control |
| C2 | Farmer carry | 3 × 30–40 m | Heaviest pair you can hold with tall posture |

Pick a weight that leaves roughly two good reps "in the tank" at the end of
each set. If form breaks down before the target reps, the weight is too heavy.

## Cool-down (5 minutes)

- 1 minute easy walking, breathing through the nose.
- Couch stretch or half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, 45 seconds each side.
- Doorway chest stretch, 45 seconds.
- Child's pose, 60 seconds.

## Progression

Use **double progression**: keep the weight the same until you hit the top of
the rep target on every set with good form, then go up to the next dumbbell and
start again at the bottom of the range. Example for goblet squat: work 4 × 8,
then 4 × 10, then 4 × 12 at the same weight; next session, heavier dumbbell at
4 × 8. Log each session in the Workout page so the PR tracker sees the trend.
If you stall for three sessions in a row on the same lift, check sleep and food
before adding volume.
